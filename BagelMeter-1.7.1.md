# Bagel Meter 1.7.1

- Every account shows when usage was actually checked. Failed or old readings no longer look current, including credits and forecasts.
- Multiple profiles using the same account share one allowance row. All profiles and history stay available.
- Sign-in opens your usual browser, checks the expected account, and only then updates its saved login. Wrong-account and failed logins no longer claim success.
- Stalled sign-ins have a clear recovery message. Pending sign-ins survive an app restart.
- Phone and computer sharing distinguish fresh usage from a freshly received message. Update other Macs, the Windows helper and phone companion for the full freshness display.
- Reconnecting a duplicate profile shows its own progress or error alongside the healthy account’s usage.
