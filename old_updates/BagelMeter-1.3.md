# Bagel Meter 1.3

- New **My devices** tab: see which of your accounts are signed in on each of your computers, and whether each is open now or when it was last used. Add a computer from that tab.
- Each account now says where it's in use: open on this Mac, used here 12m ago, on your other computers, or "used elsewhere" when usage went up and none of your computers touched it (your phone or the web).
- **Hide 5-hour** checkbox next to Compact.
- Updates now show as a blue dot on the 🥯 and an "Update to …" button, instead of a window that could open behind everything.
- If you open Bagel Meter from Downloads, it offers to move itself to Applications so updates and Launch at login work.
